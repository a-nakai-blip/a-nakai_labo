# Nakai Laboratory Website (English / Japanese)

The English homepage is `index.html`. Japanese pages are in `ja/`.

## GitHub Pages
1. Upload the contents of this folder to the repository root (not the enclosing folder).
2. Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` → `/(root)`.
3. The site is published at `https://a-nakai-blip.github.io/a-nakai_labo/`.

## Pages
- `index.html` — English homepage
- `publications.html` — English publications overview
- `basketball.html` — English men's basketball page
- `ja/index.html` — Japanese homepage
- `ja/publications.html` — Japanese publications overview
- `ja/basketball.html` — Japanese men's basketball page
- `style.css` — shared styles

## Editorial notes
- The official faculty database currently lists 2 books/book chapters, 52 academic papers, and 109 conference presentations.
- Complete bibliographic records should be checked against the official database before adding translated citations.
- Student names and research topics require confirmation of preferred public names and consent.
- Confirm official club name, schedule, venue, league affiliation, and results before adding specific details.

## Bilingual structure
- English home: `index.html`

- Japanese publications: `ja/publications.html`
- English basketball: `basketball.html`

The Japanese and English pages are intended to carry the same research areas, projects, member information, and publication records. Publication titles shown in English are translations for international readers; check the official university research database for original Japanese titles and authoritative bibliographic details.

Journal-title corrections:
- `Leisure & Recreation` is the J-STAGE English title for 自由時間研究.
- `Journal of Leisure and Recreation Studies` is the parallel English title for レジャー・レクリエーション研究.
- `Osaka Research Journal of Physical Education` is the official English title for 大阪体育学研究.
- `Bulletin of Kyoto Bunkyo Junior College` is the parallel title for 京都文教短期大学研究紀要.
- `The Clinical Gait Analysis Forum of Japan` is the English title used for 臨床歩行分析研究会誌.
- `Journal of Running Science` is the English title for ランニング学研究.
- `Computer & Education` and `Journal of the Japan Society of Technology Education` are retained as official journal titles.
