ABOUT THE TEAM

About the Club

Pursuing athletic development and personal growth through sport.

Through daily practice and competition, club members work to improve individual skills and physical fitness while developing teamwork, initiative, communication, and responsibility. Team goals and the club’s approach are shaped and shared with the members.
